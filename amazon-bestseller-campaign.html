Campaign FAQ

Do you guarantee bestseller status?

No. Lupi does not guarantee rankings, sales, category placement, or marketplace outcomes.

When should planning start?

Earlier is better. Campaigns work best when the book page, audience, reviews plan, and launch assets are ready before the push.

Can an existing book run a campaign?

Yes, but the plan may look more like a relaunch or targeted promotion than a first-release campaign.
