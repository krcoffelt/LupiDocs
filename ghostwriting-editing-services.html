Ghostwriting and editing FAQ

Can ghostwriting still sound like me?

That should be the goal. Strong ghostwriting uses the author's voice, experience, and perspective as the source.

What if I only have notes?

Notes, recordings, outlines, and interviews can all become source material for a manuscript.

Do I need editing after ghostwriting?

Often, yes. Drafting and editing are different phases, and both protect the reader experience.
