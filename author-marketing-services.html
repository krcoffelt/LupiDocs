Author marketing FAQ

Is author marketing only for business authors?

No. Memoirists, speakers, coaches, fiction writers, and experts can all benefit from a clearer author platform.

Should the author or book be promoted first?

Usually both. The right balance depends on whether the book supports a brand, story, mission, or commercial offer.

Can this support speaking or consulting?

Yes. A strong book can make an author's expertise easier to understand and share.
