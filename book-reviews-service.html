What ethical review outreach looks like

A review program begins with a book that is ready to be evaluated and a list of readers whose interests match it. Support can include identifying appropriate advance readers or review channels, preparing outreach language, organizing copies and follow-up, and monitoring which requests receive a response.

Reviewers must be free to share an honest opinion. Lupi does not sell positive Amazon reviews, require a particular rating, or use undisclosed incentives. Authors should disclose material relationships and follow the current rules of every retailer and review platform.

A review-ready book has

  • A stable edition and accurate retail page.
  • A clear description and intended audience.
  • A legitimate way to deliver reader copies.
  • Outreach that asks for honesty, not praise.
