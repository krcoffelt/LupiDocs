Manuscript review services

A manuscript review helps authors understand what is working, what needs attention, and what should happen before editing, design, or publication. Lupi Docs & Designs reviews manuscripts with publishing goals and reader experience in mind.

This is useful when you are too close to the draft to see the gaps, when you need an outside publishing perspective, or when you want to decide whether the manuscript is ready for the next phase.

Structure

Review organization, flow, chapter order, and whether the manuscript delivers a clear journey.

Clarity

Identify places where the reader may need stronger context, transitions, or examples.

Readiness

Clarify whether the next step is revision, coaching, editing, design, or publishing planning.

Manuscript review FAQ

Is this editing?

A manuscript review is higher-level feedback. It helps determine what kind of editing or development may be needed next.

Can you review an unfinished draft?

Yes. Early review can help prevent wasted effort and clarify the best structure before the manuscript grows.

Is this only for nonfiction?

No. Manuscript review can help memoirs, business books, novels, and expertise-driven books.
