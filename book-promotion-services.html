Book promotion FAQ

Is promotion different from marketing?

Promotion is often the campaign activity inside a broader marketing plan. It turns strategy into visible reader outreach.

Can you promote books already on Amazon?

Yes. Published books can be promoted through relaunch strategy, review outreach, messaging updates, and new campaigns.

Will promotion guarantee a bestseller?

No. Lupi can support ethical campaign planning and momentum, but rankings and sales are controlled by the marketplace and readers.
