Book publishing FAQ

Do I need a finished manuscript?

No. Lupi can help at the idea, outline, draft, manuscript review, design, launch, or already-published stage.

Is this traditional publishing?

No. The focus is author-centered support for self-publishing and independent publishing paths.

Can you help with marketing too?

Yes. Publishing and marketing are connected, especially when the book supports a business, story, or public platform.
