For authors who want a publishing partner

Publishing a book involves connected decisions. The audience affects the manuscript and positioning. The final manuscript affects cover and interior design. Metadata, retail setup, launch timing, reviews, and promotion all work better when they support the same promise to readers.

This service is the most hands-on Lupi path. It is designed for authors who want coordinated help rather than a list of disconnected vendors or a generic publishing package. The exact scope is built around the book’s stage, audience, goals, and existing assets.

Authors keep their voice, rights, and royalties. Lupi provides services and project guidance; it does not require authors to surrender ownership of their work.

This may fit when you…

  • Have a strong idea but need a realistic route to publication.
  • Want help coordinating editorial, design, and production decisions.
  • Need the book and launch plan to support the same audience.
  • Prefer founder-led attention over a one-size-fits-all process.

What a scope can include

  • Audience, positioning, format, and project roadmap
  • Manuscript review, development, coaching, or editorial coordination
  • Cover direction, interior layout, and production assets
  • ISBN, metadata, category, pricing, and retail-readiness guidance
  • Launch planning, ethical review outreach, and book promotion
  • Author-platform or relaunch support when the book is already published

What it does not promise

No responsible publishing service can guarantee bestseller status, sales volume, reviews, media placement, or a specific marketplace result. Lupi’s work focuses on preparation, quality, positioning, and a practical path to readers.

Your proposal should identify the agreed deliverables, responsibilities, and next decision points so the work is clear before production begins.

Book publishing FAQ

Do I need a finished manuscript?

No. Support can begin with an idea, outline, partial draft, complete manuscript, or an already-published book.

Is this traditional publishing?

No. Lupi provides author-centered publishing services. The author keeps ownership rather than submitting the book for a traditional publishing contract.

Can I choose only part of the process?

Yes. If you need guidance rather than coordinated execution, consider publishing consulting. If you want to remain more hands-on, explore self-publishing support.
