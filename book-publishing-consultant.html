Consulting FAQ

When should I talk to a consultant?

Before committing to production decisions, during a stuck writing phase, or before relaunching a published book.

Can consulting lead into done-for-you support?

Yes. Consulting can clarify whether you need coaching, design, marketing, or broader publishing support.

Is consulting only for new books?

No. Existing books often need fresh positioning, metadata, review strategy, or promotional planning.
