Clarity before committing to a full scope

Authors often reach a point where every possible next step sounds urgent. One vendor recommends editing. Another recommends a new cover, advertising, reviews, or a complete relaunch. A consultant helps separate what the book actually needs from what can wait.

Consulting is an advisory service. It is not automatically a done-for-you publishing package. The goal is to examine the project, explain the tradeoffs, and organize the next decisions into a roadmap you can use whether Lupi supports later work or you take the plan elsewhere.

If you already know you want coordinated execution across several stages, begin with book publishing services. If you intend to remain hands-on but need support for selected stages, consider self-publishing support.

Consulting can help when…

  • You have an idea or manuscript but no publishing sequence.
  • You received conflicting recommendations from vendors.
  • You need to decide whether the manuscript is ready.
  • Your published book is not reaching the intended audience.
  • You want a second opinion before a larger investment.

Publishing consulting FAQ

When should I speak with a consultant?

Before committing to an expensive production stage, when the project is stuck, or when an already-published book needs a more honest assessment.

Will consulting include editing or design?

Not unless those deliverables are explicitly included in a separate scope. Consulting identifies needs and priorities; production services carry out the work.

Does a roadmap guarantee results?

No. A roadmap improves decision quality and preparation, but sales, reviews, rankings, and publishing outcomes depend on many factors outside any consultant’s control.
