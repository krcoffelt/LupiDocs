Book design FAQ

Can design start before editing is final?

Cover direction can start earlier, but final interior layout usually needs a stable manuscript.

Do print and ebook need different files?

Usually yes. Print and ebook formats have different technical and reading requirements.

Can design help marketing?

Yes. A strong cover and clean interior make the book easier to present to readers, reviewers, and partners.

Design decisions start before decoration

The cover must communicate genre, tone, and reader expectations at thumbnail size while also meeting the printer’s full-wrap specifications. Interior design turns the final manuscript into a readable system for headings, paragraphs, images, tables, page numbers, front matter, and back matter.

Useful inputs include the intended audience, comparable books, trim size, formats, page count, image requirements, printer or distributor, and final edited manuscript. Print, ebook, and promotional assets should be planned as related but distinct deliverables.

Production review should check

  • Typography, hierarchy, margins, widows, and page breaks.
  • Image resolution, bleed, color expectations, and permissions.
  • Spine width, barcode area, trim, and platform templates.
  • A digital proof and at least one physical proof before approval.
