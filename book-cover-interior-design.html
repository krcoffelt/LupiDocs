Book design FAQ

Can design start before editing is final?

Cover direction can start earlier, but final interior layout usually needs a stable manuscript.

Do print and ebook need different files?

Usually yes. Print and ebook formats have different technical and reading requirements.

Can design help marketing?

Yes. A strong cover and clean interior make the book easier to present to readers, reviewers, and partners.
