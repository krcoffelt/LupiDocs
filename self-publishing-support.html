You remain the publisher

Self-publishing puts the author in charge of the rights, files, accounts, and final decisions. That freedom is valuable, but it also means someone must connect dozens of choices: what the book promises, which editorial work comes next, how it should look, where it will be available, and how readers will discover it.

Lupi’s role is to bring structure and professional support to the pieces you do not want to navigate alone. You can request focused help for one stage or coordinate several stages around a shared plan.

This path differs from full book publishing services: the author stays more actively involved in decisions and may already have editors, designers, files, or platform accounts in place.

A good fit for

  • First-time authors who want a guide through unfamiliar decisions.
  • Experienced authors who need help with selected production stages.
  • Entrepreneurs connecting a book to a larger brand or offer.
  • Published authors preparing a cleaner relaunch or new edition.

Build the right scope

Support can begin wherever the book is now

Before the manuscript is final

Clarify the reader and book promise, organize an outline, identify manuscript gaps, and determine whether coaching, review, ghostwriting, or editing is the right next investment.

Before design and upload

Coordinate cover direction, interior needs, trim and format decisions, metadata, ISBN planning, and the assets required for print and ebook publication.

Before launch

Prepare positioning, retail-page content, categories, keywords, timing, ethical review outreach, author messaging, and a practical launch sequence.

After publication

Assess the book page and current assets, identify gaps in reader trust or positioning, and plan updates, reviews strategy, promotion, or a relaunch.

Self-publishing FAQ

Do I keep my rights and royalties?

Yes. The service is designed around author ownership. Your written agreement should clearly describe services and deliverables rather than transferring ownership of the book.

Can I use my own editor or designer?

Yes. Existing contributors and completed assets can be incorporated into the plan after they are reviewed for the next stage.

Can you guarantee sales or reviews?

No. Lupi can improve readiness, presentation, planning, and outreach, but marketplace outcomes and reader responses cannot be guaranteed.
