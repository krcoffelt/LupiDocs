Independent publishing

Self-publishing support for authors

Self-publishing gives authors control, but it also creates decisions around editing, design, files, ISBNs, retail pages, categories, pricing, launch timing, and marketing. Lupi Docs & Designs helps authors make those decisions with a clear process.

The goal is not to take ownership of your book. The goal is to help you publish with more confidence, stronger presentation, and a practical plan for reaching readers.

What support can include

  • Book positioning and audience clarity
  • Manuscript review and editorial direction
  • Cover and interior design coordination
  • Launch, review, and author marketing planning

Who it helps

First-time authors, entrepreneurs, memoir writers, consultants, and published authors who want a better relaunch can all use self-publishing support to avoid scattered decisions and weak launches.

Self-publishing FAQ

Can I keep my rights?

Yes. Lupi's support is designed around authors keeping control of their book, rights, and royalties.

Can you help if I already uploaded my book?

Yes. Published books can still benefit from better marketing, reviews strategy, metadata, design improvements, or relaunch planning.

Do you handle every step?

The scope depends on the author and project. Some authors need strategy; others need hands-on production and launch support.
