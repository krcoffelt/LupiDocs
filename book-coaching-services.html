Book coaching FAQ

Is book coaching the same as ghostwriting?

No. Coaching guides the author through the writing process. Ghostwriting is more hands-on writing support.

Can coaching help with an outline?

Yes. A strong outline is often the first step toward a manuscript that can actually be finished.

Who is coaching best for?

Authors with expertise, life stories, or partial drafts who need structure and accountability.
