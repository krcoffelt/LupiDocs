Book marketing FAQ

Can marketing help after launch?

Yes. Many books need a second push with better positioning, review strategy, and reader outreach.

Do you guarantee sales?

No ethical book marketer can guarantee sales. The work focuses on readiness, visibility, and consistent outreach.

Is author marketing part of book marketing?

Often, yes. For business books, memoirs, and expert-led books, the author's platform is part of the marketing engine.

What a book marketing plan can cover

The scope begins with the intended reader and the book’s strongest reason to matter. From there, Lupi can help align the description, author message, retail presence, launch calendar, email and social content, review outreach, partnership opportunities, and paid or earned promotion.

The goal is not to be everywhere. It is to choose the channels the author can sustain and give each activity a measurable job: awareness, qualified visits, purchase interest, reviews, referrals, or invitations.

Before promotion starts

  • Confirm the reader promise and comparable titles.
  • Audit the cover, description, formats, and purchase links.
  • Identify existing audience assets and realistic outreach capacity.
  • Set a launch or relaunch timeline with clear responsibilities.
